# Refined media drop folder

Upload your new photos and videos here. This folder is not part of the website, it's just a holding spot so the files can be sized and wired into the site.

**How to upload:** on GitHub, open this folder, click **Add file > Upload files**, drag your files in, and commit.

**Helpful to include**
- Real before and after shots of the same car, same angle (two files per car).
- Photos of your own jobs: driveway and garage detail shots, interiors, wheels, paint close-ups.
- Short clips (up to about 10 seconds, portrait or landscape) of you working.
- A higher-resolution or transparent version of the logo, if you have one.

**Naming:** anything is fine, but a short note helps, for example `before-corolla-1.jpg`, `after-corolla-1.jpg`, `polishing-bonnet.mp4`.

**Sizes:** photos straight from a phone are fine. Videos can be large, so keep each one under 100 MB (GitHub's limit per file).

**Don't upload:** customer number plates or faces without their OK, or photos you don't own.
