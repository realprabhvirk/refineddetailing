Local Conditions

South-east Queensland is hard on paint. Here's how we deal with it.

Sun

Summer UV and heat

Clear coat fades and chalks under our sun. A sealant or ceramic coating takes the punishment instead of your paint.

See ceramic coating →
Mud and sap

Storm mud and tree sap

It bakes in and dulls the finish. Clay decontamination lifts it, and a sealant makes the next wash quicker.

See Exterior Refresh →
Damp

Humidity and mould

Damp carpets and trim turn musty. We treat trim, carpets and seats for mould, then dry them out.

See Interior Reset with mould treatment →
