Ceramic coating

Ceramic coating without the sales pitch.

Here's what a coating does, what it doesn't, and what you pay for each one. Prices are fixed and include GST and travel to your door.

Price your coating or call 0444 546 092

What it does

A hard, slick layer over your paint.

  • Mud, bugs and bird droppings wipe off more easily.
  • Water beads and sheds.
  • Gloss stays sharper.
  • UV has a tougher barrier to get through.

That last one is the reason to do it here. South-east Queensland sun fades and chalks clear coat. A coating takes that punishment instead of your paint.

What it doesn't

It's not armour.

  • It won't stop stone chips or deep scratches.
  • It isn't maintenance-free. You still wash the car.
  • If anyone tells you otherwise, they're selling something.

Ceramic coating is for cars, SUVs, utes and 4WDs. We don't offer it for motorbikes.
